# Druid — meta notes (June 2026)

## Current decks
- **Merithra / Azshara / Hostage Druid:** ramp + value/combo variants. Cheat on mana,
  then drop oversized threats or a swing finisher earlier than normal.

## Key threats / cards to play around
- **Ramp into early big threats:** expect a big body / swing turn ahead of curve. Hold
  removal for the payoff rather than spending it on small stuff.
- **Big armor / sustain (some variants):** racing a Druid that gains armor is hard —
  pressure early before they stabilize, or grind if you out-value.
- **Swing/finisher turns:** identify the deck's payoff and play around it (don't walk
  into the big AoE/board-in-a-card or the combo turn).

## Mulligan / early game vs Druid
- Keep proactive early plays to punish their slow/ramp starts; keep answers for big
  single threats. Apply pressure before their payoff comes online.

## Win condition reminder
- Usually: pressure their life total early (ramp decks are soft before their payoff),
  or out-grind value variants. Falling behind on tempo lets them ramp safely.
