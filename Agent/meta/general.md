## Top decks to expect on ladder (June 2026)
- **Demon Hunter** — Broxigar Demon Hunter is a top-tier aggressive/tempo deck (often #1 legend). Expect fast, wide pressure and burst reach.
- **Druid** — multiple strong variants: Merithra Druid, Azshara Druid, Hostage Druid. Ramp into big swing turns / value finishers.
- **Hunter** — "No Hand Hunter" (empties its hand for payoffs); aggressive, reach/burn to close.
- **Death Knight** — BBU "Herald" Death Knight; board + value.
- **Warlock** — Renathal "Tick Tock" Warlock (40-card control/value).
- **Mage** — Quest Mage (combo/value; can assemble a burn or value finish).

## Universal "play around" reminders
- **Aggro (DH, Hunter):** their clock is fast. Prioritize board control, taunts, and
  your own life total. Don't durdle for value if you're getting raced — stabilize first.
- **Control / 40-card (Warlock, some DK):** they win the long game. Apply pressure,
  force their removal/AoE with lesser threats before committing your key minion, and
  watch for sweepers — don't overextend into an obvious board clear.
- **Combo / Quest (Quest Mage, combo Druid):** they want to reach a specific turn.
  Pressure their life total to force defensive plays and shorten the game; deny tempo.
- **Ramp (Druid):** they cheat out big threats early. Have answers ready and pressure
  before their payoff turns come online.

## When unsure
- If you don't recognize the archetype yet, read the opponent's already-played cards
  in the state and infer. Early: assume the most popular deck for that class (above).
