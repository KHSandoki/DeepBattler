# Death Knight — meta notes (June 2026)

## Current decks
- **BBU "Herald" Death Knight:** board-centric value deck. Builds a board, leverages
  rune payoffs, and grinds with sustained pressure + removal.

## Key threats / cards to play around
- **Sustained board + value:** they refill and trade well. Don't overcommit into their
  removal; force it with lesser threats first.
- **Self-damage / lifesteal swings:** DK often pays life then heals back — racing their
  face can be a trap if they have lifesteal/armor.
- **Corpse payoffs:** the longer the game, the more their engine pays off; close before
  they snowball value.

## Mulligan / early game vs DK
- Keep board-contesting cards and efficient removal. Aggression that ends the game
  before their value engine dominates is good.

## Win condition reminder
- Beat them on tempo before the grind, or out-value if you're the more controlling
  deck. The slow game usually favors the DK.
