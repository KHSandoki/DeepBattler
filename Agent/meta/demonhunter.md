# Demon Hunter — meta notes (June 2026)

## Current decks
- **Broxigar Demon Hunter:** top-tier aggressive/tempo. Fast, wide pressure plus burst
  reach to finish. Often the deck to beat.

## Key threats / cards to play around
- **Burst reach + attack buffs:** DH can swing for a lot in one turn (hero attack,
  cheap buffs, weapons). Respect lethal from a seemingly safe life total.
- **Wide tempo boards:** they flood and attack. Clearing the board > racing face;
  AoE and taunts are excellent.
- **Outcost them early:** they want to curve out and snowball; breaking their tempo
  on turns 2-5 is often the game.

## Mulligan / early game vs DH
- Keep cheap removal, taunts, healing, and on-curve contesting bodies. You must not
  fall behind early.

## Win condition reminder
- RACE / tempo war. Survive the early snowball and stabilize the board; if you reach
  the mid-game with a stable board and some life buffer, you're usually winning.
