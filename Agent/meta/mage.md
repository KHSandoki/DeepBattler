# Mage — meta notes (June 2026)

## Current decks
- **Quest Mage:** completes a quest for a powerful payoff; combo/value plan that can
  assemble a burn or value finish. Also expect tempo/spell-damage variants.

## Key threats / cards to play around
- **Burn to the face:** Mage closes with direct damage (e.g. Fireball-class spells +
  hero power pings). Track their potential burst and stay out of range late.
- **Freeze + tempo:** freezing your attackers buys them time; plan attacks before they
  can freeze, and don't rely on a single big attacker.
- **The quest/combo payoff:** identify their wincon turn and pressure their life total
  to force them to play defense instead of comboing.

## Mulligan / early game vs Mage
- Keep early pressure (Quest/combo Mage is soft early), and sticky threats. Race the
  combo; don't give them a slow, safe game.

## Win condition reminder
- Apply pressure before the quest/combo comes online, and respect lethal burn from
  hand once you're in range. Slow games favor their value/burn finish.
