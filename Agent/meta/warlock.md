# Warlock — meta notes (June 2026)

## Current decks
- **Renathal "Tick Tock" Warlock:** 40-card control/value. Extra life total, heavy
  removal and card advantage, wins the long game.

## Key threats / cards to play around
- **Board clears / AoE:** the #1 thing to respect. Don't overextend — develop just
  enough to keep pressure, hold reinforcements for after the sweeper.
- **40 cards + healing/armor:** their effective life total is huge. Pure go-face racing
  is hard; you need reach or relentless, clear-resistant pressure.
- **Card advantage / value engines:** they out-grind you long-term. Apply a fast,
  sticky clock; don't let the game go late.

## Mulligan / early game vs Warlock
- Keep proactive, sticky early plays and reach. You want to threaten lethal before
  their removal + life total takes over.

## Win condition reminder
- Pressure FAST and play around the clear. If the game goes long, the 40-card control
  deck almost always wins the attrition.
