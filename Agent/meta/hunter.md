# Hunter — meta notes (June 2026)

## Current decks
- **No Hand Hunter:** dumps its hand fast for payoffs, then refuels. Aggressive board
  + reach/burn (e.g. Kill Command-style direct damage) to close from mid-range.

## Key threats / cards to play around
- **Reach/burn to the face:** Hunter can close from ~8+ off-board. Don't sit at low
  health assuming you're safe — keep out of burst range when you can.
- **Wide boards / beasts:** they go wide and buff. AoE / efficient trades are strong;
  a single big body is worse than clearing their board.
- **Secrets:** if they have a secret up, sequence around the common Hunter secrets
  (e.g. attack with a small/expendable minion first, play around freezing/snipe traps).

## Mulligan / early game vs Hunter
- Keep early removal, cheap bodies, and anything that contests the board on curve.
  Defensive/healing/taunt cards gain value. Toss slow/expensive cards.

## Win condition reminder
- This is usually a RACE. Stabilize the board and protect your life total; if you
  fall behind on board you take burst + reach and lose. Trade efficiently, then stabilize.
